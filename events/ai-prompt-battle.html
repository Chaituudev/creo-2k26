Quick Snapshot

  • Multi-round timed prompt battle.

Rounds / Structure

How it runs

  • High-stakes, multi-round prompt battle with strictly timed turns.
  • Participants receive 3 prompts per round to generate the most accurate or creative outputs.
Entries & Fee

Slots

  • Entries: Limited seats available on a first-come basis.
  • Fee: ₹125/- per entry.
Rules & Regulations

Play fair

  • Open to solo participants or teams of maximum 2.
  • Adhere strictly to the 3-prompt-per-round time limit.
  • Usage of only approved LLM tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Gemini) is allowed.
  • All prompts must be non-plagiarized and crafted on-the-spot.
  • Internet usage for research is prohibited outside the designated tools.
